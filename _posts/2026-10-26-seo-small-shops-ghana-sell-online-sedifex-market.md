## Step-by-Step Guide for Sellers

### 1. Create Your Online Store on Sedifex Market

Visit [Sedifex Market](https://www.sedifexmarket.com) and sign up to create your store. You’ll be guided through adding your shop name, description, and product listings. Keep your product descriptions clear and include photos to attract buyers.

### 2. Add Your Products and Manage Inventory

Add products one by one, including prices and descriptions. Sedifex Market allows you to update stock levels instantly. This means when an item sells, your inventory lowers immediately, helping you avoid selling items that are out of stock.

### 3. Set Up Your Payment Methods

Link your preferred payment options through [Pay Sedifex](https://pay.sedifex.com). This integration enables customers to pay instantly online and receive receipts immediately. It’s a secure and straightforward setup that suits Ghanaian payment habits.

### 4. Promote Your Store

Share your Sedifex Market store link on social media, WhatsApp groups, and within your community. The more visibility you create, the higher your chance of attracting buyers.

### 5. Manage Orders Efficiently

When customers place orders, you’ll receive notifications. Access your seller dashboard on Sedifex Market to view and process these orders. Confirm availability, prepare the items, and coordinate delivery or pickup as agreed.
