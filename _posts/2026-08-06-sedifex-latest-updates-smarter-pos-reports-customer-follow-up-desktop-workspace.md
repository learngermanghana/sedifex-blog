# Sedifex Latest Updates: Smarter POS Reports, Faster Customer Follow-Up and a Better Desktop Workspace

Running a business becomes easier when your software gives you clear information and helps your team act quickly. The latest Sedifex updates focus on three important areas: **better sales reporting, stronger customer management and a more comfortable desktop experience**.

These improvements are designed for shops, service providers, schools, salons, restaurants and other businesses that use Sedifex to manage daily operations.

## 1. Clearer Discount Details in POS Sales Reports

Businesses need to know not only how much they sold, but also how discounts affected each transaction.

Sedifex POS sales reports now show clearer discount information, helping business owners and staff understand:

- the original sale amount;
- the discount applied;
- the final amount paid by the customer; and
- the true value of discounted sales.

Discount details are also included in report exports. This makes it easier to review sales outside Sedifex, prepare internal reports and compare promotions over time.

For example, when a shop runs a weekend promotion, the owner can clearly see whether the discount increased sales without confusing the original price with the final amount received.

## 2. Newly Saved Customers Are Easier to Track

Customer information is valuable when it can be used for follow-up, support and future sales.

Sedifex now makes newly saved customers easier to identify. This helps staff quickly notice recent additions instead of searching through the full customer list.

The system also avoids marking existing customers as new, making the tracking more accurate.

This improvement can support practical tasks such as:

- welcoming a new customer;
- confirming their details;
- following up after a purchase;
- sending information about a service or promotion; and
- building a cleaner customer database.

## 3. Faster WhatsApp Contact and Customer Export

Many Ghanaian businesses communicate with customers through WhatsApp. Sedifex has improved customer contact tools so staff can move more easily from a saved customer record to direct communication.

Businesses can also export customer contact information when they need to organise outreach, prepare a contact list or keep an approved business record.

Customer data should always be used responsibly. Messages should be relevant, professional and sent with the customer’s permission.

## 4. Improved Contact Detection

Sedifex has also improved how customer contact details are recognised, including cases where a customer provides an email address without a phone number.

This reduces incorrect contact handling and supports businesses that work with different types of customer records.

## 5. A More Spacious Desktop Workspace

Some business tasks require more screen space, especially when reviewing reports, managing records or working with detailed pages.

Sedifex now makes the full-width desktop workspace option easier to notice. Users can expand their working area and reduce unnecessary visual distractions when completing important tasks.

This is especially useful for staff who spend long periods using Sedifex on a laptop or desktop computer.

## What These Updates Mean for Businesses

Together, these changes help businesses work with greater clarity and speed.

A manager can review discounted sales more accurately. A staff member can quickly identify a newly saved customer. A customer-service team can follow up through familiar communication channels. An administrator can use more screen space when working with reports.

The goal is simple: help businesses spend less time searching for information and more time making good decisions.

## How to Get the Most from the New Improvements

To benefit from these updates, businesses should:

1. Review the POS sales report after each business day.
2. Check discount details before evaluating a promotion.
3. Keep customer names and contact information accurate.
4. Follow up with new customers professionally.
5. Use the wider desktop workspace for reports and detailed administrative work.
6. Export business data only when necessary and store it securely.
