## Step-by-Step Guide for Small Shops to Sell Online

### 1. Sign Up on Sedifex Market

Head over to [Sedifex Market](https://www.sedifexmarket.com) and create your seller account. The registration process is straightforward and designed for busy small business owners.

### 2. Set Up Your Online Store

Add your shop’s name, location, and profile picture. This helps customers recognize your brand.

### 3. Add Your Products or Services

Upload clear photos and descriptions of your products or services. Include prices and any variants (sizes, colors).

### 4. Manage Inventory

Update your stock levels regularly to avoid selling items that are out of stock. Sedifex Market’s platform allows you to adjust quantities easily.

### 5. Enable Online Checkout

Activate online payment options via [Pay Sedifex](https://pay.sedifex.com) to allow your customers to pay securely with mobile money or cards.

### 6. Monitor Orders

Check your order dashboard often. Sedifex Market shows new and pending orders so you can prepare shipments or service appointments promptly.

### 7. Provide Instant Receipts

Once a customer completes a payment, they receive an instant receipt. This builds trust and improves your shop’s professionalism.

### 8. Communicate with Customers

Use the messaging tools on Sedifex Market to answer questions, confirm orders, or update delivery info.
