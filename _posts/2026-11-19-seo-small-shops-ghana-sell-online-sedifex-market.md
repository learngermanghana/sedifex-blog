## Step-by-Step Guide to Selling on Sedifex Market

### 1. Register Your Shop
Visit [Sedifex Market](https://www.sedifexmarket.com) and sign up as a seller. Provide basic information about your shop, services, or products. Registration is straightforward, designed for small business owners without needing IT expertise.

### 2. List Your Products or Services
Add details of what you sell with clear descriptions, prices, and photos. Accurate product information helps buyers make informed decisions. You can update your listings anytime, which is useful for salons or boutiques with changing inventory or promotions.

### 3. Set Up Online Checkout
Sedifex Market integrates with [Pay Sedifex](https://pay.sedifex.com) to provide a secure and simple online payment system. Buyers can pay instantly online, which speeds up transactions and reduces cash handling risks for your shop.

### 4. Manage Orders with Store Visibility
Once your shop is live, you can view all orders directly from your seller dashboard. Store order visibility allows you to track new, pending, and completed orders in real time. This helps you stay organized and respond promptly to customers.

### 5. Update Inventory Regularly
Keep your product or service availability up to date on Sedifex Market. This avoids situations where buyers order items that are out of stock or no longer available. You can easily adjust your inventory levels through your seller dashboard.

### 6. Provide Instant Receipts
Each online transaction generates an instant receipt for the buyer and seller. This receipt serves as proof of purchase and helps you maintain clear records, improving trust and professionalism.
