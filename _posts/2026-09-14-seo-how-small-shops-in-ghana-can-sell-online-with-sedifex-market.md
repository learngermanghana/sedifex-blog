## Step-by-Step: How to Start Selling on Sedifex Market

### 1. Register Your Shop

Head over to [Sedifex Market](https://www.sedifexmarket.com) and sign up for an account. Registration is straightforward and free, designed with small businesses in mind. You’ll create your shop profile by adding your shop name, location, and contact details.

### 2. List Your Products

Upload clear photos of your products with detailed descriptions and prices. Whether you sell clothes, salon services, handmade crafts, or food items, accurate product information helps buyers make informed choices.

### 3. Set Up Your Payment Method

Connect your bank account or mobile money wallet to receive payments instantly via [Pay Sedifex](https://pay.sedifex.com). Sedifex Market supports multiple payment options commonly used in Ghana to make checkout easy for customers.

### 4. Manage Orders and Inventory

Once your shop is live, you’ll receive notifications for every order placed. Use your Sedifex Market dashboard to view orders, update status, and communicate with buyers if needed. Keep your inventory updated to avoid selling items out of stock.

### 5. Promote Your Online Shop

Share your Sedifex Market shop link on social media, WhatsApp groups, or through flyers to attract customers locally and nationwide. Sedifex Market also helps increase your visibility by listing your shop in category searches.
