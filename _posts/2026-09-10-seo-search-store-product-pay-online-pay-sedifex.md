## For Buyers: How to Search and Pay Online

### 1. Searching for Stores or Products on Sedifex Market
Start by visiting [Sedifex Market](https://www.sedifexmarket.com), a trusted marketplace connecting buyers with local Ghanaian sellers. You can use the search bar to enter keywords related to the product you want or the store you prefer. For example, searching "fresh tomatoes Accra" or "clothing stores Kumasi" will give you relevant results.

Filters are available to narrow down options by price, category, location, and seller rating. This helps you find exactly what you need without scrolling endlessly.

### 2. Viewing Product Details and Store Profiles
Once you find a product or store, click to view detailed descriptions, prices, and seller information. This transparency helps you make informed decisions. Sellers often upload photos and list inventory details to ensure accurate availability.

### 3. Adding Items to Your Cart and Proceeding to Checkout
When you’re ready, add items to your cart. You can continue shopping or proceed directly to checkout. At checkout, you’ll see a summary of your order, including quantities and total cost.

### 4. Paying Securely with Pay Sedifex
To complete your purchase, select Pay Sedifex as your payment method or go directly to [Pay Sedifex](https://pay.sedifex.com). Here’s what you need to do:

- Provide your payment details safely on the platform.
- Confirm the payment amount.
- Submit your payment.

Pay Sedifex supports popular Ghanaian payment options and ensures your transaction is encrypted for security.

### 5. Instant Receipts and Order Confirmation
Immediately after payment, you will receive an instant receipt via email or SMS. This confirms your order and payment have been processed successfully. You can also check your order status anytime on your Sedifex Market account.

### 6. Tracking Your Order and Inventory Updates
The store will be notified of your purchase and can update their inventory to reflect your order. You can communicate directly with the seller for any questions or delivery arrangements.
